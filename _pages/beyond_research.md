In research as in life, I believe that placing the human being at the centre of the design is essential. For this reason, my commitment to understanding the world and having a positive impact is not limited to rigorous academic research.

Together with other tireless fellows, I work to nurture and improve the community where I grew up and where I hope to return permanently soon: Paesana, in Valle Po (Cuneo, Italy). We are engaged in designing and carrying out social and cultural projects ranging from urban regeneration to a festival, as well as trail maintenance and local promotion.

The project I am most proud of is **<span style="color: var(--global-theme-color);">Zana Fest</span>**, a music, culture, and sports festival held across the Valle Po. It places physical, mental, and social wellbeing at its centre, with the aim of offering people a concrete reason to stay, return, or grow in Valle Po.

Putting the human being at the centre means building bonds grounded in shared values and intentions. In doing so, even in a territory facing the typical challenges of inner areas and mountain regions, I believe we are building a credible and sustainable alternative: one where staying in the valley is a choice, not the absence of one.
