I am a PhD candidate in Economics at **Università Cattolica del Sacro Cuore**, supervised by [Arianna Galliera](https://sites.google.com/view/ariannagalliera), [Selene Ghisolfi](https://sites.google.com/view/seleneghisolfi/), and [Fabrizio Panebianco](https://sites.google.com/site/fpanebianco/fabrizio-panebianco?pli=1&authuser=0).

I am interested in understanding how **inequalities** are transmitted through individual decision-making, and how **technology** reshapes effort, delegation, and compensation.

To study this, I use **microeconomic theory** and **experimental economics**.

**Current research interests**:

- Microeconomic theory
- Experimental economics
- Inequality
- Technology and work
